Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains how ToolNova handles information when you use our website.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ToolNova. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit and use ToolNova and its online tools.

By using ToolNova, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information you provide to our tools

Some ToolNova tools allow you to enter text, upload files or provide other information to perform a task.

Where a tool is designed to process information directly in your browser, that information can be processed locally without being uploaded to a ToolNova server.

You should always review the behavior of a particular tool and avoid entering confidential or highly sensitive information into any online service unless you are comfortable doing so.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

ToolNova itself may use basic technologies needed to operate and improve the website.

Third-party advertising or other services displayed on the website may use cookies, identifiers or similar technologies according to their own policies.

4. Advertising

ToolNova may display advertisements from third-party advertising providers.

These providers may collect or receive certain information, such as browser, device or advertising-related information, in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

ToolNova does not control the privacy practices of third-party advertising providers.

5. Third-party services

Some ToolNova features may rely on third-party libraries, content delivery networks or other external services.

Such services may process technical information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Analytics and technical information

ToolNova may use analytics or similar technologies in the future to understand website usage and improve the service.

This may include general technical information such as browser type, device type, approximate location, referring pages and pages visited.

7. Data security

We aim to use reasonable measures to protect the website and information associated with it.

However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's privacy

ToolNova is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

9. External links

ToolNova may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page by updating the date or relevant content.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact page .
