About PDF Page Extractor

This tool lets you create a new PDF containing only the pages you need from an existing document.

Example

If your PDF contains 10 pages and you enter 1, 3, 5-7, the resulting PDF will contain pages 1, 3, 5, 6 and 7.

How to use it

  1. Select your PDF file.
  2. Enter the page numbers or ranges.
  3. Click Extract Pages.
  4. Download the new PDF.

Your PDF is processed directly in your browser.
